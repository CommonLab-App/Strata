# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Topics replace weeks in everything the learner sees: routes are `/topic/<slug>` (old `/week/<n>` links redirect), titles drop the number, Home lists numbered topics, the nav item is "Topics", and the copy no longer says "this week". Internals keep the week names (src/app/weeks.ts explains).
- Fix: the plate-boundaries layer is now `plate-boundaries`; the Protomaps basemap style owns a layer called `boundaries`, so weeks 5 and 8 threw "Layer already exists" and drew no boundaries once the self-hosted basemap went live. `.htaccess` lets other origins read `basemap/`, so a local preview can run on the live copy.
- Self-hosted basemap goes live: the `basemap/` folder from `scripts/build-basemap.sh` (Protomaps zoom 0 to 8 extract, 549 MB) is uploaded beside the app and excluded from the deploy rsync so `--delete` never removes it; `.htaccess` caches `.pbf` glyphs and the `.pmtiles` archive for a week.
- Deploy fixes from the first Lighthouse run on the live site: icon files are mode 644 and the deploy rsync sets modes, so the host serves the manifest icons; `public/.htaccess` gives hashed assets a year-long immutable cache, the worker and shell no-cache, and the manifest its media type; the attribution link rule now outranks MapLibre's stylesheet. docs/DECISIONS.md records the week-8 scores (71/96/96/100).
- Offline mode (phase 6, docs/specs/phase-6-polish.md): a service worker built from `src/sw/worker.js` by a Vite plugin precaches the shell, every bundle asset and the fonts under a cache named by their hash, serves same-origin requests cache first and navigations network first, and caches sample media as it is watched; data APIs and tiles are untouched (the localStorage snapshots are the offline data). A web app manifest with the maskable icons makes the app installable. `scripts/deploy.sh` rsyncs a build to the host named in `.env`.
- Core sets match the hand samples the owner holds: 14 minerals and 21 rocks are `core`; the rest are marked extra. The identification labs draw from the core sets (week 2 without gypsum) with smaller trays, so weeks 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 move to stored-entry version 2 and stored trays from version 1 are regraded. Limestone is labelled micrite.
- Week 18 built: the globe can wear another planet's raster tiles (NASA Trek mosaics of the Moon and Mars), the crater-counting engine (`src/engine/craters/`), the Moon's craters of 20 km and above bundled from the Robbins (2018) database (`scripts/fetch-craters.mjs`), the planet Explore, the two-box crater-counting lab, Learn text and ten questions. Every week now has content; the Draft list is empty.
- Week 17 built: the structure engine (`src/engine/structure/`: lines, poles, great circles, equal-angle projection, plane intersection, seeded tasks), an SVG stereonet, the two-plane Explore with the fold axis, the read-the-net lab, Learn text and ten questions.
- Week 16 built: the infinite-slope engine (`src/engine/slope/`: factor of safety, failure angle by bisection, three soils, seeded tasks), the slope-stability sandbox, the failure-angle lab, Learn text and ten questions.
- Week 15 built: the surface models (`src/engine/surface/`: a valley profile rounded under ice with a shape exponent, flooded fraction of a grid), a coastal elevation tile added to the terrain bundle, the Surface Sandbox Explore (glacier sliders and profile, sea level on the coast with flooded cells drawn blue), the U-valley and flooded-coast lab, Learn text and ten questions.
- Week 14 built: the water adapters and gauge picker take a kind (stream or well, parameter 72019 for depth to water), the groundwater engine (`src/engine/groundwater/`: Darcy, Thiem, an inverse-distance water table with contours and flow direction, seeded tasks), the well Explore with a Darcy sandbox, the water-table lab, Learn text and ten questions.
