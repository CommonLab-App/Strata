Brand kit v1.0

A visual system for StrataLab, the free, open-source geology learning app. Built from the one drawing every physical-geology course opens with: a block diagram of a normal fault.

Deep time, close up. · stratalab.app · November 2026

01

The idea

Strata are the subject, so strata are the brand: five coloured beds in textbook lithology colours, drawn flat with a dark outline, the way they appear in a lab manual. The mark cuts those beds with a single normal fault. The hanging wall on the right has dropped half a bed, so every boundary steps. That step is the whole identity in one shape: the ground has a history, and you can read it.

Everything else follows from that drawing. The dike that cuts the hanging wall is the accent colour, Magma, which also colours “Lab” in the wordmark. The slab serif has horizontal bars for the same reason the beds do. Tags and readouts are set in a monospaced face because the app is a lab, and labs print data.

Principles

  • Textbook, not texture. Flat fills, one outline weight, no gradients or shadows on the logo. Lithology hatching (brick, dots, dashes) appears only on illustrations and the textured mark at large sizes.
  • Geologically honest. Hanging wall down, slickenlines down-dip, dike younger than the beds. If a drawing would make a geologist wince, change the drawing.
  • Observational voice. The app shows live Earth data and never interprets risk. The brand speaks the same way: it points at the record and lets you read it.
  • Sibling of Valence. Same shell, same Inter body face, same free-and-open stance. Different mineralogy.
02

The mark

Anatomy

  • Canvas 0 0 128 128, corner radius 28, outline 3 px inside (Basement).
  • Five beds, 24 units tall, cycling Sandstone → Shale → Limestone → Mudstone → Greensand from the top.
  • Fault trace from (46,128) to (82,0), about 74° dip, stroke 5 units, Basement.
  • Throw: the hanging wall (right) drops 12 units, half a bed, so every contact steps. A full-bed throw would make the beds line up again and hide the fault.
  • Minimum size 16 px. Below 48 px use the plain mark, never the textured one.
  • Clear space: half the mark's height on every side.

Variants

Colour works on both light and dark backgrounds because the outline carries it on light and the beds carry it on dark. Mono variants are a single ink with the beds and fault cut out, for one-colour print, embossing and favicon fallbacks.

Don't

Don't rotate the mark, flip it (that reverses the sense of slip) or redraw the fault as a dike (red). Don't recolour the beds to match a theme; they are the identity. Don't put the mark on a mid-tone background where neither the outline nor the beds have contrast. Don't add a drop shadow, gradient or bevel.

03

Wordmark and lockups

The wordmark is Zilla Slab Bold with slightly tightened tracking, “Strata” in ink and “Lab” in Magma. It is outlined to paths in every SVG, so no font needs installing. In the horizontal lockup the mark stands a little taller than the cap height and is centred on it; in the stacked lockup the mark is 1.7× the type size. The tagline is JetBrains Mono 500, uppercase, 0.14 em tracking, at 70–78% ink.

Bed order, top to bottom

The sequence is fixed. Illustrations and the mark always start with Sandstone at the surface and cycle in this order, so every drawing in the app reads as the same section.

  1. Sandstone
  2. Shale
  3. Limestone
  4. Mudstone
  5. Greensand

In the UI

Primary buttons stay neutral (Slate on light, Chalk on dark), as in Valence. Magma is reserved for links, the active nav item, progress and focus rings, so it always means “this is live or clickable”. Strata colours are for data: chart series, lithology swatches, week markers. Limestone is too pale for text; use it only as a fill.

Contrast at a glance: Magma on Chalk 4.7:1 (fine for links and UI, use 600 weight for small text); Magma-bright on Basement 5.1:1; Slate on Chalk 13.1:1.

06

Illustration

The house drawing style is the filled block diagram: cabinet projection with depth running up and to the right, flat lithology fills with hatching on the front face, shaded side faces, one outline weight in Basement, half-arrows for slip. It is the style of the screenshot this kit started from, redrawn with the brand beds. Use it for module headers, empty states and the About page; the labelled version is a teaching figure in its own right.

Rules for new drawings

  • Beds in the fixed order, 46-unit spacing at hero scale; hatching: Limestone brick, Sandstone dots, Shale dashes, Greensand ticks, Mudstone solid.
  • Outlines 1.6 units at 90% Basement; the fault trace 2.6 units. Side faces are the bed colour mixed 22% toward Basement; top faces are the surface bed mixed 42% toward Chalk.
  • Exposed fault planes are Shale mixed halfway to Chalk with Shale-Deep slickenlines down-dip.
  • Intrusions are Magma. Nothing else is.
  • Reverse and thrust faults are welcome as new figures; keep the sense of slip correct and the arrows on both walls.
07

Voice

Tagline

Deep time, close up.

Observational and a little uplifting: the app brings billions of years to arm's length. Alternates, for places the primary has already been used: Read the layers. · The ground keeps records. · Every contact is a date.

Tone

  • Plain textbook register. Name the thing, then say what it tells you.
  • Never alarmist. Live quakes and floods get a neutral caption and a source link; the brand never says “danger”, “threat” or “risk”.
  • Numbers carry units and a timestamp. Always.
  • No course numbers, institution or campus names, or places described in our own words. Query, cache, cite.

Status-line voice

Loading and progress text is lowercase, present-tense, monospaced, and ends in a blinking underscore. It describes the geology being done, not the software.

reading the section fetching the last hour of quakes resolving the crystal lattice correlating beds across the fault cached 14:02 UTC · USGS

Naming

StrataLab, one word, capital S and L. Never “Strata Lab” or “STRATALAB” in prose. The domain is written lowercase: stratalab.app. Weeks are “Week 7 · Structural geology”, module names in Zilla Slab.

08

Social and app

Wiring it into the app

  1. Replace public/favicon.svg (it is still Valence's molecule) with app/favicon.svg; copy favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon-180.png and the maskable icons into public/ and reference them in index.html and the manifest.
  2. Paste the :root and .dark blocks of tokens/stratalab-brand.css over the neutral ones in src/index.css. Variable names match shadcn's, plus --brand and the raw --strata-* palette.
  3. npm i @fontsource/zilla-slab @fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono; import the 600 and 700 Zilla faces and set --font-heading and --font-mono in @theme inline.
  4. Use header-lockup-*.svg in the shell at 32 px height, swapping on .dark.
  5. Drop the labelled block diagram on the About page beside the credits.
